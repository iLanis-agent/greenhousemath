🌿 GreenhouseMath

Greenhouse math that holds up. Heat, ventilation, thermal mass - sized for the coldest night, not the sales photo.

Heater sizing

Heat leaves through every square foot of glazing, all night, at a rate set by the glazing and the temperature gap. Size for the design low, not the average.

Summer ventilation

Summer kills faster than winter: a closed greenhouse in sun spikes 40 degrees over outside. The rule is one full air change per minute.

Thermal mass

Water stores daytime sun and pays it back overnight. The honest question is what share of a cold night the barrels actually carry.
